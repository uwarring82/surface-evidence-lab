# Rights and distribution

No blanket licence is assigned to this bundle. A licence and attribution owner for the original project text and small verification script remain to be selected by the project lead; a `LICENSE` file is intentionally not fabricated. Repository setup can proceed while that decision remains recorded.

Third-party material retains its source-specific terms. The [manifest](literature/source-manifest.md) records the rights basis, version, source URL, inspection limits and retained bytes for each item.

| Material | Recorded terms / packaging decision |
|---|---|
| Seibert article | CC BY 4.0 according to the preserved Bielefeld/NOAH rights record; keep that provenance record with the PDF |
| Croshaw article and supplement | CC BY 4.0; retain attribution and original notices |
| Pálinkás article and data record | CC BY 4.0 as recorded; check any figure-specific third-party notices before reuse; the dataset ZIP is absent |
| Xu article XML | CC BY-NC 4.0; stored in `local-source-archive/`; linked reading and original commentary in teaching core |
| Lu unchanged PDF | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; stored in `local-source-archive/`; linked reading and original commentary in teaching core |
| Other leads | Links/metadata and commentary only; their full sources have not been relicensed or included |

`sources/` includes the article copies and associated provenance records selected for retention in the repository. A source article's licence does not automatically license an entire repository landing page or unrelated third-party item; the preserved HTML is evidence of the recorded rights assignment, not a blanket licence statement.

The full handoff ZIP includes the two local archival files so the existing preservation work is retained. `.gitignore` excludes that directory's contents, except its explanatory README, from a normal initial commit. Their citations, rights and checksums remain in the repository manifest. This is the chosen separation between source preservation and the public teaching core; it is not a general interpretation of all NC/ND uses.

Future attributed Seibert/Croshaw figure layouts must name the source and licence, retain relevant credit lines and state modifications. No such finished layout is included yet. Source bytes are unchanged; Xu caption corrections remain in original commentary and are not a publisher erratum.
