# Rights and distribution

No blanket licence is assigned to this bundle. A licence and attribution owner for the original project text and small verification script remain to be selected by the project lead; a `LICENSE` file is intentionally not fabricated. Repository setup can proceed while that decision remains recorded.

Third-party material retains its source-specific terms. The [manifest](literature/source-manifest.md) records the rights basis, version, source URL, inspection limits and retained bytes for each item.

| Material | Recorded terms / packaging decision |
|---|---|
| Seibert article | **Redistribution permission unverified.** The earlier CC BY 4.0 claim was withdrawn on 17 September 2026: the preserved "rights record" is a browser-verification page, and the PDF carries an ACS copyright notice. PDF stored in `local-source-archive/`; linked reading and original commentary in teaching core until permission for the exact version and figure is verified |
| Croshaw article and supplement | CC BY 4.0; retain attribution and original notices |
| Pálinkás article and data record | CC BY 4.0 as recorded; check any figure-specific third-party notices before reuse; the dataset ZIP is absent |
| Xu article XML | CC BY-NC 4.0; stored in `local-source-archive/`; linked reading and original commentary in teaching core |
| Lu unchanged PDF | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; stored in `local-source-archive/`; linked reading and original commentary in teaching core |
| Other leads | Links/metadata and commentary only; their full sources have not been relicensed or included |

`sources/` includes the CC BY article copies and associated provenance records selected for retention in the repository, plus the failed Seibert rights retrieval kept as evidence. A source article's licence does not automatically license an entire repository landing page or unrelated third-party item; the preserved HTML is evidence of the recorded rights assignment, not a blanket licence statement.

The full handoff ZIP includes the Xu and Lu archival files and the Seibert PDF so the existing preservation work is retained. `.gitignore` excludes the contents of `local-source-archive/`, except its explanatory README, from Git. Their citations, rights and checksums remain in the repository manifest. This is the chosen separation between source preservation and the public teaching core; it is not a general interpretation of all NC/ND uses.

The Seibert PDF was briefly public in this repository on 17 September 2026 under the withdrawn claim. The repository was made private and its history rebuilt without the PDF; the rebuilt history replaces the original repository. Copies downloaded or cached while it was public cannot be recalled.

Future attributed Croshaw figure layouts must name the source and licence, retain relevant credit lines and state modifications. Seibert figures are not reproduced until permission for the exact version and figure is verified and recorded. No such finished layout is included yet. Source bytes are unchanged; Xu caption corrections remain in original commentary and are not a publisher erratum.
