Record at least one claim of each relationship kind, with at least one supported claim and one relationship genuinely unresolved by the supplied source. Do not satisfy the latter with unknown chemical identity alone. Split additional events and claims into further rows.

Leave unknown measurement dates unknown. The date you edit the table is not a measurement or publication date. Lu (2014) and Seibert (2020) illustrate differently dated interpretations of comparable phenomena; they do not revise a claim that two exact records show the same region. Mark any hypothetical revision as hypothetical.
