# Scope a research pilot around acquisition and physical retention

Priority: deferred research transition. Owner: unassigned. Status: no lab or pilot specimen committed.

Define a pilot that can distinguish bulk diffusion from ambient contamination through physical observables and controls. A confidence label alone is not the result. Test whether the source lab benefits immediately from acquisition-record and deposition tooling.

Review specimen identifiers/lineage, retention and disposal, treatment/exposure/transfer/storage history, time in air, stage information, calibration and beam/probe dose or interaction records relevant to the selected instruments. State what must be captured at acquisition and what is unknowable retrospectively.

Verify named reuse leads (NOMAD/Oasis, NeXus modality definitions, NFFA metadata, PSDI AFM collection) against a sample export. Specify institutional operating/retention responsibility; software availability does not establish a durable archive. Select/propose a standards profile before inventing instrument metadata.

Acceptance: physical hypothesis/control design, partner benefit and feasibility note, minimum acquisition record, specimen retention plan, candidate deposition/profile fit and assigned implementation responsibility. Identify which longitudinal/gap mechanics must pass U1/U2 before use, and the additional validation needed for quantitative registration. No lab contact is implied by this issue draft.
